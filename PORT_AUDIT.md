### Frozen player during possession

A staged browser check in the shipped sewer (level ID 24) successfully charged an orb below the unchanged ceiling turrets and transferred control to a turret. Andou subsequently died and the level restarted, preventing a meaningful firing/release check. Source comparison identified a missing original behavior: Android's FROZEN animation has no frames and clears drawing and collision volumes, whereas the port skipped animation updates during ghost control and retained the charge-frame hitboxes.

The port now selects an empty frozen animation during possession and the return-camera delay. SpriteComponent clears collision volumes and draws nothing for an explicit empty animation, then restores the selected body's art and volumes on release. The shipped-sewer regression test charges and steers into a turret without teleporting the orb, runs enemies/projectiles for ten seconds, checks unchanged player health and continued control, and verifies body/volume restoration after release. A separate sprite test covers empty-animation clearing and restoration.

Browser verification of the corrected build subsequently passed two takeover/fire/release cycles on the same rightmost sewer turret. The fixture resets/refills and positions Andou at x1408 on the shipped floor, without moving enemies or changing their AI/terrain. Normal charge input and the canvas orb pad perform the takeover; Fly fires and Stomp releases. Read-only observations recorded five muzzle shots in the first 1.5-second firing interval and five more after re-possession, all four idle/attack frame names, two deliberate releases, and unchanged player health of three. Andou's body and camera return were visible on release; no console errors/warnings were captured. This supersedes earlier table entries describing turret takeover/firing/release as browser-unverified. It remains a staged encounter, not a complete sewer route or a brobot possession playthrough. The original turret orientation is fixed; it cannot be aimed with the directional controls.

### Stomp landing recovery and effects

The original holds STOMP for 0.15 seconds after landing, shakes the camera for that duration, and emits two stationary mirrored dust puffs at the feet. The port immediately cleared the stomp flags, making the later Game.tsx shake branch unreachable; dust was never spawned. Landing now owns its effects and recovery inside PlayerComponent, and stomp ignores steering/jet input until recovery finishes. The dust factory uses the original five 24 FPS frames, EFFECT priority, flip directions and 0.3-second lifetime. The obsolete late shake branch was removed.

`playerStompLanding.test.ts` drives the actual player onto a solid surface using input, checks one shake and two stationary mirrored puffs, the protected recovery interval, restored movement and vulnerability, particle removal, and a second input-driven jump/stomp without resetting player state. Ordinary landings emit no stomp effects. Existing staged block/boss collision tests now reset the player controller between their separately staged attacks rather than carrying over a completed landing. Those tests are not full route/fight playthroughs. Browser presentation verification remains pending.

### Save reset storage isolation

`resetEverything` directly deleted the browser localStorage key before updating the Zustand store, bypassing the configured storage adapter. This could delete an unrelated browser save during isolated tests and threw when browser storage access was denied despite a working configured backend. Reset now replaces the complete persisted payload through the store's own adapter, without a direct localStorage deletion.

The new isolated-process `savePersistence.test.ts` / `tests/save-round-trip.ts` regression failed at the direct deletion before the fix and now passes. It serializes real store actions, clears only live memory, and rehydrates through Zustand to check level continuation/unlocks, attempts, best score/time, diaries, scores, extras, statistics and settings. It also verifies progress-only reset preserves settings, full reset persists defaults, and neither touches a separate browser-storage sentinel. This is serialization/hydration verification, not an interactive browser reload or erase-dialog check. No actual user save was erased.

Latest checks: **422 tests pass across 86 files**, typecheck and lint pass, production build passes with the existing large-bundle warning. These tests do not substitute for complete interactive campaign verification.
